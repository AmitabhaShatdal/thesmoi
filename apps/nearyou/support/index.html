Common questions

How does Near You work?

You choose your own contact card and the contacts to keep track of. When two people have each other saved and both use Near You, each phone can tell whether the other person is within the “nearby” distance you set, and can send you a notification when they arrive. The app does not show anyone a map or coordinates.

Why can’t I see anyone nearby?

Check each of these:

  • Both of you need the app, and each of you needs the other’s number saved in your contacts and added in Near You. Sharing only works when it is mutual.
  • Your own contact card needs your mobile number, and you need to have chosen your country code.
  • Location should be set to Always and Precise Location turned on (iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Near You), so your phone can notice when you move to a new area.
  • Contacts access should be allowed, and notifications if you want alerts.
  • You need an internet connection. If you just installed the app, open it once and give it a minute.
  • If “Go Quiet” is on, your phone is not sending new locations.
Why does Near You ask for “Always” location?

So your phone can notice when you have moved a long way, even when the app is closed, and then update the people you are mutual contacts with. Near You uses the low-power system monitoring that wakes the app when you have travelled roughly 20 km (12 miles), rather than tracking you continuously. Without “Always”, your location cannot be shared while the app is in the background.

How do I stop sharing my location?
  • Go Quiet: tap the account button in the top-right corner of the main screen, open Privacy, and turn on Go Quiet. Your phone stops sending new locations.
  • Remove a contact to end the link with that person. Sharing only works while you have each other saved.
  • Turn off location for Near You in iOS Settings.
  • Delete your account (below) to remove everything.
How do I delete my account and my data?

In the app: tap the account button in the top-right corner of the main screen, open Privacy, and tap Delete Account. This deletes your account and everything stored for it, on your phone and on our server. It cannot be undone. Near You does not change anything in your phone’s Contacts app when you delete your account.

If you cannot open the app, email hidden@icloud.com from the address you can verify and tell us the phone number on your account, and we will help.

See the Privacy Policy for details of what is deleted.

I have a new phone. What do I do?

Delete your account in the app on your old phone first (Privacy › Delete Account), then set up Near You on the new phone with the same number. If you no longer have the old phone, email us and we will help.

Does it use a lot of battery?

No. Near You relies on low-power system monitoring and sends an update only when you have moved a long way, not constantly.

Someone added my number and I don’t use the app.

When a Near You user adds a contact, only that contact’s phone number is stored on our server, with no name or other details. If you would like your number removed, email hidden@icloud.com.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone with iOS 15 or later.

How do I report a problem or abuse?

Email hidden@icloud.com. If you feel unsafe, turn on Go Quiet, remove the contact in question, and contact local authorities if needed.
