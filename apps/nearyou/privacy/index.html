Privacy Policy

Near You · Last updated October 29, 2026

The short version
  • Near You tells you when people you know are in the same area. It only works between two people who have each other saved in their contacts.
  • To do that, the app sends your phone number, the phone numbers of the contacts you add, and your location to our server.
  • We never show your location on a map to anyone, we do not sell your data, there is no advertising, and the app has no analytics or tracking.
  • You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

1. Who we are

Thesmoi (“we”, “us”) runs the Near You app and the server behind it. You can reach us at hidden@icloud.com.

What stays on your phone: your contacts’ names, emails, addresses, birthdays, notes, photos and social handles; the “nearby” distance you choose; and the list of which contacts are near you.

What we do not collect: advertising identifiers, analytics, or usage tracking. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics code. Our server software does not record IP addresses, although our hosting provider may keep ordinary network logs.

3. How your location is shared

Your location goes to our server. The server passes it only to the phones of your mutual contacts: people who have saved your number and whom you have saved. Their app uses it to work out whether you are inside the distance they chose, and may notify them that you are nearby. The coordinates are delivered to those phones for the app to use; the app does not display your coordinates, a map, or distances.

To control this:

  • Go Quiet (account button › Privacy) stops your phone from sending new locations. The last location we received stays stored until you send a new one or delete your account.
  • Removing a contact ends the link with that person.
  • You can turn off location for Near You in iOS Settings.

4. Permissions

  • Contacts: so you can choose your own card and the contacts to keep track of. Only the phone numbers of the contacts you add are sent to us. To match numbers across countries, Near You asks for your country code and may add it to local numbers in your Contacts app; it tells you before it does so.
  • Location (“Always”): so your phone can notice when you have moved a long way while the app is closed. Near You uses low-power system monitoring for this, not continuous tracking.
  • Notifications: to tell you when a contact is nearby.

You can decline or later withdraw any of these in iOS Settings. Features that depend on them will then not work.

5. Services we use

  • Apple: push notifications (Apple Push Notification service), App Attest, and Apple’s location and maps services, which look up addresses you enter or that are saved in your contacts. Apple’s own privacy policy applies to those services.
  • Our hosting provider, which runs the server.

6. Sharing

We share your information only (a) with your mutual contacts’ apps, as described in section 3; (b) with our hosting provider so it can run the service; and (c) where the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for advertising.

7. How long we keep it, and how to delete it

We keep your information while your account exists.

  • Delete Account (account button › Privacy › Delete Account) removes your contact list, push token, key and last location from our server and from your phone. Your mutual contacts’ apps are told you no longer have a location. Near You never changes your phone’s Contacts app when you delete your account.
  • If you delete the app without deleting your account, we remove the account automatically once we find that your device can no longer receive our notifications.
  • A phone number may remain in our number index as an entry with no name, location or account information while other people have it saved. You can ask us to delete it by emailing hidden@icloud.com.
  • Server backups are kept for up to 30 days and then deleted.

8. Security

Connections to our server are encrypted. Requests are signed with a key that never leaves your device, and the server checks that they come from a genuine copy of the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Most of this you can do directly in the app (see section 7). For anything else, email hidden@icloud.com; we will reply within 30 days. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

10. Children

Near You is not intended for children under 13 (or under 16 where the law of your country sets a higher age for consent), and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has used the app, email us and we will delete the account.

11. Where your information is processed

Our server is located in the United States. By using Near You you understand that your information is processed there.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
