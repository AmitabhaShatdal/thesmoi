Near You

An iPhone app for friends, family and contacts.

Near You tells you when someone you know is in the same area as you, so you never miss the chance to meet up when you are both in town.

How it works

  1. Pick your own contact card and the contacts you want to keep track of.
  2. It only works when both of you have saved each other. If you have each other in your contacts and both use Near You, the app can tell when you are in the same area.
  3. You choose how close counts. Set your own “nearby” radius and get a quiet notification when someone arrives.

You stay in control

  • The app never shows your location on a map to anyone.
  • There is no advertising and no tracking.
  • “Go Quiet” stops your phone from sending new locations, and you can delete your account and data from inside the app at any time.
